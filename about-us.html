About Us

Welcome to Harichand Steel, where innovation blends with durability to define true excellence since 2004.

Since 2004

Experts Dedicated to Your Success

We are trusted steel gate fabricators based in Delhi, specializing in premium steel gates, aluminum profile gates, and rolling shutters. Established in 2004, Harichand Steel has spent more than two decades perfecting metal fabrication, delivering architectural excellence for luxury homes, residential estates, and commercial buildings.

Our commitment to quality, modern design, and precision craftsmanship has made us a preferred choice for durable and stylish gate manufacturing across Delhi and Delhi NCR.

Durability

Engineered to resist harsh weather, corrosion, and heavy daily usage without sagging or fading.

Strength

Fabricated using heavy-gauge SS304 stainless steel, reinforced aluminium sections, and solid MS frameworks.

Trust

Thousands of satisfied homeowners and builders who rely on our prompt delivery and honest factory pricing.

STRONG FOUNDATION

State-of-the-art welding, CNC profiling, and automated gate motor integrations.
