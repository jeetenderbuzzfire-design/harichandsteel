## Why Choose Aluminium Profile Gates?

            

              Traditional iron gates are prone to rust and require frequent repainting, while heavy solid gates place
              immense stress on automated motors and hinges. Aluminium profile gates have become the preferred choice
              for Janakpuri homeowners because:
            

            

              - **Zero Rust & Corrosion:** Aluminium naturally forms a protective oxide layer, making
                it completely impervious to rainwater, moisture, and sunlight.

              - **Lightweight Operation:** Puts 60% less strain on gate hinges and automation motors,
                ensuring decades of silent, smooth movement.

              - **Contemporary Aesthetics:** Available with sleek architectural slats, wood-sublimation
                finishes, and luxury rose gold pull handles.

              - **Eco-Friendly & Low Maintenance:** Easy to clean with plain water and 100%
                recyclable.
