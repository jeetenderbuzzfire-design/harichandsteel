## Types of Steel Gate Designs

            

              Steel gates have evolved far beyond basic metal bars. At Harichand Steel, our fabrication workshop
              produces multiple distinctive gate design styles tailored to varying architectural languages:
            

            

              - **Modern Steel Gate:** Minimalist horizontal slat gates, integrated flush sheets, and
                contrasting metallic handles that match contemporary villa architecture.

              - **Laser Cut CNC Steel Gates:** Intricate geometric, floral, or abstract patterns cut into
                heavy gauge steel plates with computerized precision.

              - **Aluminium Profile Steel Framework:** Combining the structural rigidity of steel with
                the lightweight elegance and rust immunity of aluminium profiles.

              - **Double Door Swing Gates:** Classic majestic entrances ideal for wide driveways and
                bungalow entrances.

              - **Sliding Motorized Steel Gates:** Space-saving sliding track designs compatible with
                automatic remote sensors and mobile app controls.

              perforated slat patterns maintain air flow without compromising protection.
